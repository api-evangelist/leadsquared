Feature Overview The LinkedIn Ads Lead Sync Connector captures lead data from LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms configured in your LinkedIn account and uses it to create new leads in your LeadSquared account. This helps you capture leads automatically and follow up with them faster to improve conversion rates.
