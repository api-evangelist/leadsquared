1. Feature overview Distribution Engine is a simple, rules-based way to automatically assign Leads (Objects), Opportunities, and Tasks to the right users. It replaces complex automation-based assignment with an easy-to-manage setup that helps you distribute records fairly, reduce manual effort, and meet response SLAs.
