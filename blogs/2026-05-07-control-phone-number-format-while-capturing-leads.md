1. Feature Overview The Phone Control setting in LeadSquared helps standardize how phone numbers are captured across your lead forms and landing pages. When enabled for the Phone/Mobile Number field, it restricts users to enter only numeric values and allows you to define the minimum and maximum number of digits.
