1. Feature Overview The Manage Opportunities page shows the list of opportunities created for each Opportunity Type. On this page, users can perform actions like adding new opportunities, updating opportunities in bulk, importing and exporting opportunities, adding advanced search filters, customising visible columns, and adding new leads directly.
