1. Feature Overview LeadSquared’s Lumen Assist is an AI-powered assistant embedded directly into LeadSquared. With Lumen, you can perform CRM actions, retrieve information, and get answers to product questions through natural-language prompts, without navigating to specific pages or opening API documentation.
